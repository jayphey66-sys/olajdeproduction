Frequently Asked Questions

What is your booking process?

Fill out the contact form with your requirements. I'll review and send you a custom quote. Once agreed upon, a 50% deposit secures your date.

What's your turnaround time?

Typical turnaround is 2-3 weeks for edited images. Rush services available for additional fees.

Do you offer custom packages?

Absolutely! Every project is unique. Contact me to discuss your specific needs and budget.

What about usage rights?

All clients receive full commercial usage rights to their images. You'll own the digital files.

Do you offer retouching?

Yes! Professional retouching and post-processing are included in all packages.

Can you shoot on location?

Yes! I offer on-location shoots. Travel fees may apply depending on distance.

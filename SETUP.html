🎥 OlajideProduction - Quick Setup Guide

⚡ Quick Start (5 minutes):
  1. Replace images in assets/images/ with your photos
  2. Update your info (email, phone, address) in all HTML files
  3. Open index.html in your browser
  4. Deploy to GitHub Pages or Netlify (see below)

Step 1: Add Your Images

Replace placeholder images in assets/images/:

  • Portfolio images: 8 images (500x500px) - portfolio-1.jpg to portfolio-8.jpg
  • Blog images: 6 images (600x400px) - blog-1.jpg to blog-6.jpg
  • Shop images: 9 images (400x400px) - product-1.jpg to product-9.jpg
  • About image: 1 image (500x600px) - about-1.jpg

Tip: Compress images with TinyPNG.com for faster loading.

Step 2: Update Your Information

Open each HTML file and replace:

  • OlajideProduction → Your photography business name
  • jayphey66@gmail.com → Your email address
  • 09169777590 → Your phone number
  • 16 Unity Close
    Hill Valley, Abule Oko Agbado
    Ogun State(Lagos State)     → Your address
  • Update social media links (Instagram, Facebook, etc.)
  • Update testimonials and about text with real client reviews

Step 3: Customize Colors (Optional)

Edit assets/css/style.css and change the color variables:

--primary-color: #2c3e50; (Main color)
--accent-color: #e74c3c; (Highlight color)

Color Ideas:

  • Professional: Primary: #2c3e50, Accent: #e74c3c
  • Warm: Primary: #34495e, Accent: #f39c12
  • Modern: Primary: #1a1a1a, Accent: #00d4ff
  • Nature: Primary: #27ae60, Accent: #2ecc71

Step 4: Set Up Contact Form

The contact form needs a backend to send emails. Choose one:

Option A: Formspree (Easiest)

  1. Visit formspree.io
  2. Create a new form with your email
  3. In contact.html, add to the form tag:
    action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID" method="POST"

Option B: EmailJS (No Backend Needed)

  1. Visit emailjs.com
  2. Set up service and template
  3. Add script and update JavaScript in assets/js/script.js

Option C: Your Own Server

Create a backend endpoint and update the fetch call in assets/js/script.js

Step 5: Deploy Online

Option A: GitHub Pages (Free)

  1. Create repository: yourusername.github.io
  2. Upload all files
  3. Your site goes live at https://yourusername.github.io

Option B: Netlify (Free, Recommended)

  1. Visit netlify.com
  2. Drag & drop your folder → Done!
  3. Get free domain or connect your own

Option C: Paid Hosting

Bluehost, GoDaddy, Hostinger, etc. - Upload via FTP

Step 6: SEO Optimization (Important!)

Update meta tags in <head> of each page:

<meta name="description" content="Your description here">
<title>Your Business - Professional Photography</title>

Example:

<title>John Smith Photography - Professional Product & Commercial Photos</title>
